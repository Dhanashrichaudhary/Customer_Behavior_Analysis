#  Customer Behavior Analysis

##  Project Overview

Customer Behavior Analysis is an end-to-end data analytics project designed to understand customer purchasing behavior, spending patterns, product preferences, subscription behavior, discount usage, and revenue contribution.

The project combines **Python (Pandas), SQL (PostgreSQL), and Power BI** to transform raw customer data into meaningful business insights and an interactive dashboard.

The analysis starts with data exploration and preparation using Pandas, followed by detailed business analysis using SQL, and finally presents the key findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

##  Project Objectives

The primary objective of this project is to analyze customer purchasing behavior and identify patterns that can help businesses better understand their customers and make data-driven decisions.

The project focuses on:

- Understanding customer demographics and purchasing patterns
- Analyzing customer spending and revenue
- Comparing purchasing behavior across genders and age groups
- Understanding subscription behavior
- Identifying repeat and loyal customers
- Analyzing product and category performance
- Evaluating discount usage
- Comparing shipping methods and customer spending
- Identifying top-performing products within categories
- Understanding revenue contribution by age group
- Creating an interactive dashboard for business reporting

##  Business Insights
The analysis of customer purchasing behavior revealed the following key business insights:

###  Customer & Subscription Behavior
- *Non-subscribers account for approximately 73% of customers, while subscribers account for **27%*.
- The high proportion of non-subscribers indicates a significant opportunity to increase *subscription adoption and customer retention*.
- Repeat purchasing behavior was analyzed using customers' *previous purchase history* to understand customer loyalty and subscription patterns.

### Revenue & Customer Spending
- *Young Adults generate the highest revenue, contributing approximately *₹62.1K**.
- *Middle-aged customers* contribute approximately *₹59.2K, followed by **Adults at ₹56.0K* and *Seniors at ₹55.8K*.
- The *average purchase amount is ₹59.76*, providing a benchmark for evaluating customer spending behavior.
- The analysis of revenue by age group shows that *younger customer segments are slightly stronger contributors to revenue*.

###  Product & Category Performance
- *Clothing is the highest-selling category, with **1,717 purchases*.
- *Accessories* rank second with *1,240 purchases*.
- *Footwear* accounts for *599 purchases, while **Outerwear* has *324 purchases*.
- Clothing and Accessories therefore represent the *strongest product categories by sales volume* and can be prioritized for inventory planning and marketing activities.

### Discount & Customer Behavior
- Discount usage was analyzed to identify *products with a high proportion of discounted purchases*.
- Customers who used discounts while spending above the average purchase amount were also analyzed to identify *high-value customers influenced by promotions*.
- These findings can help evaluate the effectiveness of discount strategies and support more targeted promotional campaigns.

###  Shipping Behavior
- Average purchase amounts were compared across *Express and Standard shipping* to understand whether customer spending varies according to shipping preference.
- This analysis can help businesses evaluate the relationship between *delivery preferences and customer spending*.

###  Customer Satisfaction
- The overall *average customer rating is 3.75/5*.
- This indicates a moderately positive customer experience while also providing an opportunity to identify areas where product and service quality can be improved.

### 📌 Overall Business Takeaway

The analysis indicates that *age group, product category, subscription status, discount usage, repeat purchases, and shipping preferences* are important dimensions for understanding customer behavior.

The business can use these insights to:

- Focus on high-performing product categories.
- Increase subscription adoption among non-subscribers.
- Develop targeted strategies for high-value and repeat customers.
- Optimize discount and promotional campaigns.
- Improve customer experience and satisfaction.
- Use age-based customer segmentation for targeted marketing.
- Support inventory and sales planning using category-level demand.

The findings can help businesses improve customer targeting, marketing strategies, product planning, and customer retention.
